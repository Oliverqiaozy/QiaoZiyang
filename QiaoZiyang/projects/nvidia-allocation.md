# NVIDIA Strategy, Demand & Allocation

[← Profile](../README.md)

## Business question

How can a business facing uncertain demand and constrained supply use statistical evidence to guide customer allocation?

## Team project

The group case connects four stages of analysis:

1. **Company and industry context:** examine NVIDIA's position in AI infrastructure and the role of its product and software ecosystem.
2. **Business challenges:** consider supply-chain dependencies, capacity constraints, customer concentration and competition from customers' own chips.
3. **Statistical analysis:** examine historical revenue and demand indicators, assess forecast uncertainty and question whether apparent relationships survive alternative specifications.
4. **Planning and allocation:** connect demand scenarios to feasible supply and a management response, then consider how scarce-resource governance can transfer across industries.

The project distinguishes observed case evidence, statistical estimates and proposed methods for questions that require unavailable internal data.

## Decision framework

The allocation discussion organizes management responses around protecting verified commitments, reassigning released capacity and reserving flexibility for shocks or new demand. Demand scenarios indicate different levels of pressure; they are not direct estimates of customer-level GPU quotas.

Airport-slot governance supplies an analogy for continuity and access under scarce capacity. Its numerical thresholds are not treated as NVIDIA rules or copied into a GPU allocation formula.

## Strategy and business analysis relevance

The case connects industry structure and customer dynamics with demand uncertainty, operating constraints and a management choice. It demonstrates scenario framing and the translation of analytical outputs into strategic options. It also shows where a quantitative model needs internal inputs before it can support an executable allocation plan.
